Morsel Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026. Morsel is an MCP-first, camera-first food tracker: you log meals and photos through an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, and Morsel keeps your nutrition data for you.

What Morsel stores

  • Your profile — the body metrics you set (sex, age, height, weight, activity level) and your nutrition goals.
  • Your food log — meals and items you log, the nutrition values recorded for them, and photos you upload when logging from a camera.
  • Your weight and activity history — only if you connect an Apple Health body-mass or active-energy series on iOS.
  • Your email — used to send one-time sign-in codes and to contact you about your account.

Everything is scoped to your Morsel account: each account can only read and write its own data.

Where your data lives

Morsel stores your data in Supabase (Postgres database, Auth, and object storage for photos). The Morsel service runs on Fly.io, sign-in emails are sent through a transactional email provider, and this page is hosted on Vercel. Your data is hosted by these infrastructure providers on Morsel's behalf; it is not rented, sold, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

How Morsel uses your data

  • To log, store, summarize, and return your food and nutrition data when you ask.
  • To email you the one-time codes used to sign in.
  • To respond to support requests.

Morsel shows no ads and runs no analytics or tracking. When you use Morsel through an AI assistant (for example, ChatGPT), the assistant sends Morsel only the log requests you make with your connection; Morsel never reads your conversations.

Sharing

Morsel does not share your data with third parties for marketing, advertising, or any purpose other than operating the service through the infrastructure providers listed above. Disconnecting Morsel from an assistant immediately stops that assistant's access.

Retention and your controls

Morsel keeps your account data while your account is active. You can delete individual entries in the app at any time, and you can ask Morsel to delete your data or account by emailing the address below; deletion requests are honored.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@morsel.app.
