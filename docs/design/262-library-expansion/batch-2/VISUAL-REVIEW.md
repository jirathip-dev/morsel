# Batch 2 visual review

Surface: Compare. Reviewed Paper/Night contact sheets and native 64px phone contexts for all 24 labeled studies. Generic depictions do not certify a recipe, ingredient, species, serving size or nutrition.

The first grayscale/dark-sheet review suggested 40px risks for sushi, eggplant, mince, iced coffee and Thai sweets. Inspection of the actual 64px phone contexts retained their structural cues; 40px remains diagnostic rather than the shipping criterion. No palette expansion was made.

One real native-size issue was corrected: gravy read as a cup. The prior source, exports and affected phone proofs are preserved in history/gravy-r1. The replacement is a low elongated pointed boat with a thicker, lighter descending pour. Fresh Paper and Night phone proofs show the pointed lip and stream at 64px without clipping. All other studies remained unchanged.

No missing subjects, label/row cropping, or major food-class contradictions remain in the final inspected contexts. Milk tea is orange iced tea without tapioca pearls in the editable source; boba can therefore receive a separate pearl-bearing general-coverage study later without pretending this account logged it.
