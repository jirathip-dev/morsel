## Timing discipline

source-batch snapshots. Their structural checks and hashes describe that stage;
parent corrections later supersede the Grains/Protein source hashes. Final hashes
live in the release manifest. `timing-events.json` records parent wall/monotonic
phase boundaries. An untimed local correction is disclosed, not reverse-invented
from file mtimes. `build.json`, `proofs.json`, `browser.json`, `workflow-tests.json`
and `gates.json` separate executed machine work. No export time is called an art
creation time. Screenshot and review time are not hidden inside “one command.”
